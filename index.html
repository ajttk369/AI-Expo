FAQ

Before You
Reserve

Can I buy tickets on-site?

Online reservation is recommended. On-site registration may vary depending on visitor volume.

Do programs require separate booking?

Most sessions are open to ticket holders, while selected demos or meetings may operate by queue or schedule.

Can students attend?

Yes. Student and creator-friendly sessions are included across the program and technology zones.

Is English guidance available?

Core page information supports Korean and English, with major program guidance prepared for international visitors.

Is parking available?

Venue parking is available at COEX, and public transport is recommended during peak visitor hours.
